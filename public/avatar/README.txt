Put your avatar model here as:

    avatar.glb

See AVATAR.md in the project root for the full walkthrough.

Note: Ready Player Me shut down on 31 January 2026. Use one of these instead:

  Avaturn      https://avaturn.me                     selfie -> realistic rigged GLB
  MetaPerson   https://avatarsdk.com/avatar-creator/  first avatar free
  VRoid Studio https://vroid.com                      free, stylised, desktop app

Quick version:
  1. Upload a clear, front-facing selfie
  2. Fix hair, beard, brows and glasses by hand (every tool guesses hair badly)
  3. Export GLB, save it here as avatar.glb

Bone naming does not matter - "Head", "mixamorig:Head" and similar all resolve.

Until a model is present the hero shows a portrait card. Nothing breaks.
